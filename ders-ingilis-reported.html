Reported Speech

Statements, questions, commands, backshift

Reported speech nədir?

Birinin sözünü öz dilinizlə çatdırmaq.

Direct: He said, "I am tired."
Reported: He said (that) he was tired.

Yadda saxlamağın məntiqi: hər zaman bir pillə geriyə gedir. Present → Past, Past → Past Perfect. Past Perfect isə daha geriyə getmir — olduğu kimi qalır.

Söz dəyişmələri

this → thathere → there
now → thentoday → that day
tomorrow → the next dayyesterday → the day before
ago → beforenext week → the following week

Əvəzliklər də kontekstə görə dəyişir: I → he/she, my → his/her.

Reported questions

Wh- sual:
Direct: "Where do you live?"
Reported: He asked where I lived.

Yes/No sual: if və ya whether işlənir
Direct: "Are you happy?"
Reported: She asked if I was happy.
ƏSAS QAYDA: reported question-da söz sırası düz cümlə kimi olur — sual sırası itir!
Düzgün: He asked where I lived.
Səhv: He asked where did I live.
Sual işarəsi də qoyulmur.

Reported commands and requests

  • Əmr: tell + obyekt + to + V — He told me to sit down.
  • Xahiş: ask + obyekt + to + V — She asked me to help.
  • İnkar: not to + V — He told me not to worry.

Reporting verbs — zənginləşdirmə

Hər şeyi «said» ilə deməkdən çıx — dəqiq feil seç:

suggest + V-ingHe suggested going out
promise + to VShe promised to call
refuse + to VHe refused to answer
admit + V-ingShe admitted making a mistake
apologise for + V-ingHe apologised for being late
advise + obj + to VShe advised me to rest

Backshift olmayan hallar

Zaman dəyişmir, əgər:
• Söylənən hələ də doğrudur: He said he lives in Baku (hələ orada yaşayır)
• Ümumi həqiqətdir: She said water boils at 100°C
• «say/tell» indiki zamandadır: He says he is busy
